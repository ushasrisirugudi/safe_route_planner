# 🛡️ Safe Route Planner for Women's Safety

## 📌 Project Overview

Safe Route Planner is a web-based application designed to help women travel safely by suggesting routes based on safety scores, reported risk points, and safety trends rather than only distance or travel time.

This project was developed as an IEEE Hackathon prototype to address real-world women's safety concerns during travel.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Women often face safety concerns while traveling, especially during late hours or through unfamiliar locations.

Existing navigation applications mainly focus on:

- Shortest Route
- Fastest Route
- Traffic Conditions

They do not provide sufficient information about:

- Crime-prone areas
- Poorly lit roads
- Low crowd density zones
- User-reported unsafe locations

Our solution aims to provide safer route recommendations based on safety indicators.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Safe Route Planner analyzes multiple routes and recommends the safest path using:

- Safety Scores
- Community Reports
- Risk Point Detection
- Time-Based Safety Trends
- Emergency Support Features

Users can also report unsafe locations and contribute to community safety.
